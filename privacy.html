Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. What We Collect

Nothing. myAPS does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All calculations happen entirely in your browser (client-side). Your marks are never sent to any server.

2. Cookies

myAPS uses cookies only for:

  • Cookie consent preference — stored locally to remember your choice
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics (pages visited, time on site, country). No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Google AdSense — may use cookies to serve relevant advertisements. Google's advertising cookies are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can decline all non-essential cookies via the consent banner. You can also clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — for anonymous traffic analysis
  • Google AdSense — for displaying advertisements
  • Netlify — for hosting the website

Each of these services has their own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

4. POPIA Compliance

myAPS is committed to compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no personal data to protect, share, or delete. Your marks exist only in your browser's memory and are lost when you close the tab.

5. Children's Privacy

myAPS is designed for use by students (including those under 18). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone of any age.

6. Disclaimer

myAPS is not affiliated with any South African university. Programme requirements shown are sourced from publicly available information and may not reflect the most current admission criteria. Always verify requirements on each university's official website before making application decisions.
